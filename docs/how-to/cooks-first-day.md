# How-to: a cook's first day

A walkthrough for the cook — written to be forwarded as-is. Everything
happens in WhatsApp; nothing to install.

## Morning: set up your shop (10 minutes)

1. Message the BiteFlow number: **hello**.
2. Reply **2** — "I'm a cook".
3. Reply **1** — "Set my menu".
4. Type a dish name, e.g. `Veg Pulao`, then its price, e.g. `8.50`.
5. Reply **1** to add another dish, **2** when the menu is done.

Your menu is live. Customers who message the same number now see it.

## Midday: take orders

- Each order arrives as a message: **1 👍 Accept** or **2 👎 Reject**.
- Accepting moves the order to your queue; the customer sees "Cooking 🍳".
- Update progress with **1** Cooking → **2** Out for delivery → **3** Done.
  The customer is notified at each step automatically.

## Money

- **Cash on delivery:** the customer pays at the door. Nothing to do in chat.
- **Phone transfer:** the customer sends a screenshot. You get
  **1 Approve / 2 Deny**. Only approve when the money is actually in your
  account — a screenshot is a claim, not proof.

## Afternoon: know your numbers (the 📊 Business hub)

From your home screen reply **3**:

- **1 Inventory** — log what you bought; get pinged when stock runs low.
- **2 Recipes** — teach the app what's in each dish once; it then tells
  you the food cost and margin of every dish automatically.
- **3 Procurement** — see what to buy this week based on what's selling.
- **4 Finance** — today's revenue, food cost, and margin; plus a place to
  log rent, gas, packaging, and equipment.

Every evening you get a short P&L summary automatically.

## Growing (the 📣 Marketing hub)

From your home screen reply **4**:

- **Referrals** — share your code; you and the new customer both get a reward.
- **First-order offer** — e.g. 10% off, automatically applied once per
  new customer.
- **Campaigns** — write one offer message; it goes only to customers who
  opted in.
- **Win-back** — customers who haven't ordered in 14 days get a gentle
  nudge with an offer.

## One golden rule

If a supplier raises a price, log the purchase at the new price — the app
recomputes every dish's margin and warns you *before* your next menu goes
out if any dish drops below 20% margin.
