# Video 3 script: A cook's first day on BiteFlow

**Format:** phone screen-record with voiceover, minimal talking head.
**Length:** ~8–10 min. **Audience:** a home cook, possibly a senior —
slow pace, big text, every step shown twice.
**Goal:** menu live, first order accepted, business hub tour.

---

## Scene 1 — Cold open (0:00–0:45)

**On screen:** Title card with large text — "BiteFlow: your shop, inside
WhatsApp. Nothing to install."

**Say (slowly):** "This video is for cooks. You will not install any app.
Everything happens in your normal WhatsApp, by replying with numbers —
one, two, three — or a thumbs up. Let's open your shop."

## Scene 2 — Registration (0:45–2:30)

**On screen:** Phone screen-record. Send `hello`. The welcome arrives;
reply **2**. Show the cook home screen.

**Say:** "Send hello to the BiteFlow number. Reply two — I'm a cook. This
is your home screen. Remember two numbers for today: one sets your menu,
three opens your business tools."

**On-screen text (large):** `hello` → reply `2` → you're a cook

## Scene 3 — Your first menu (2:30–5:00)

**On screen:** Reply **1**. Type dish name `Veg Pulao`, price `8.50`.
Reply **1** for another, add `Dal Tadka` / `7`, reply **2** done. Show the
"menu is live" confirmation.

**Say:** "Reply one. Type the dish name — this is the only typing you'll
do — then the price. One adds another dish, two finishes. Your menu is
live; customers see it immediately."

**On-screen text:** `1 = set menu` · `1 = add dish` · `2 = done`

## Scene 4 — First order (5:00–7:00)

**On screen:** An order arrives. Tap **1** to accept. Show the customer
getting "Cooking". Then **1 → 2 → 3** through cooking, out, done —
customer notified each time.

**Say:** "Orders arrive like this. One with a thumbs up accepts. Then one,
two, three moves the order along — and the customer is told at every
step. You never have to call anyone."

**On-screen text:** `1 👍 accept` · `1 cooking → 2 out → 3 done`

## Scene 5 — Money and the business hub (7:00–9:00)

**On screen:** Show a P2P screenshot arriving → **1 Approve**. Then reply
**3** → Business hub → **2** Recipes → a dish's food cost and margin.

**Say:** "If a customer sends a transfer screenshot, reply one to approve
— but only when the money is really in your account. And reply three any
time to see your business: what each dish costs you to make, what you
should buy this week, and tonight's earnings summary."

**On-screen text:** `approve only real money` · `3 = 📊 business hub`

## Scene 6 — Outro (9:00–9:30)

**On screen:** Title card — "Questions? Ask the person who sent you this
video. Happy cooking 🍛"

**Say:** "That's your first day: menu live, orders flowing, numbers in
your pocket. If you get stuck, reply zero any time to go back. Happy
cooking."
